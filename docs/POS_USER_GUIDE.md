# Daily workflow

The sun/moon button switches between light and dark mode. Your account → Appearance also lets you follow the device's system setting. The preference persists on this browser. On phones and tablets, the menu button opens navigation; tap outside it, use its close button, or press Escape to dismiss. Wide tables scroll horizontally within their panel. POS moves the cart below the medicine catalog on smaller screens. Invoice printing keeps a light background in either theme.

1. Sign in and select your authorized branch in the top bar. Confirm it before entering a transaction.
2. Add a supplier and medicines. Give each medicine a unique SKU and optional primary barcode; set price, tax basis points, unit and prescription classification. Archive obsolete products with the Active toggle.
3. Open Purchases, create an order with quantity and acquisition cost, then open it and receive actual quantities into the correct warehouse. Enter lot and expiry. Partial receipt leaves the order open. Only receipts create stock and supplier liability.
4. Open POS. Choose a warehouse before adding items. Search by name, generic, SKU or barcode. A USB keyboard scanner can send the barcode followed by Enter; F2 focuses search. Only eligible unexpired stock appears as available.
5. Add products, change quantities, select a customer or keep walk-in. Authorized users can set per-unit discounts within the configured limit. Add split payment rows if necessary. To leave an unpaid balance, select a named customer and enable Allow partial / unpaid sale. Collect installments later from the original invoice. Exact cash fills the payment amount. External card/bank/wallet processing must be performed separately; the application records the confirmed method.
6. Complete the sale. The server recalculates pricing, verifies payment, allocates FEFO batches and atomically saves stock, payment and journals. Cash overpayment produces change; noncash overpayment is rejected. Wait for the invoice before starting another sale. Retried identical checkout keys return the same invoice.
7. Print the invoice from sale detail. For partial returns, open the invoice, select quantities from the original batch allocations and record a reason. Confirm the return: its value reduces outstanding receivables first, then any remainder is refunded in cash. Returned medicine is always quarantined in this slice. Repeated returns cannot exceed the original quantity.
8. Use Inventory & expiry to review expired/near-expiry/quarantined batches; use Stock movements for history. Quarantine moves an entire remaining batch into controlled stock and records the reason. No release or disposal action is offered yet.
9. Accountants use Supplier payments or a purchase detail page to post partial/full supplier payments, and Expenses for paid operating costs. Authorized users return received batches to suppliers from the purchase detail page. Review the trial balance and stock reconciliation. Use Notifications after running scheduled inventory checks.

Prescription medicines cannot be checked out until an authorized prescription workflow is implemented. There is no automated medication recommendation. Parked sales, register shifts, credit limits/aging and exchange flows are pending, rather than silently simulated.

See [partial payments and purchase returns](PAYMENTS_AND_PURCHASE_RETURNS.md) for settlement rules and permissions.
